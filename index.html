HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

  • SPACE or CLICK drop the block
  • hold SHIFT slow-mo (refills by itself)
  • A anchor: freeze the sway for 3 s
  • C camera · P pause · M mute

LANDING

  • PERFECT centred: combo, calmer tower, slow-mo refill
  • GOOD close enough
  • RISKY the offset makes the tower lean
  • Over half a block off and it slips. That costs a life.
