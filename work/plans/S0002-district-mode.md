# S0002: District mode and the fictional city

**Type:** feature
**Status:** approved
**Triage:** ready-for-agent
**Source:** `work/instructions/S0002-district-mode.md` (decisions of two grilling rounds), S0001 plan, ADR 0002.

## Problem Statement

A city is one tower with a target height. Finishing it does not feel like building a city, and nothing in the game
shows the player's city growing. The developer wants a city-building step that leads to the next city, but it must be
our own mechanic, not the grid-and-neighbour city mode of an existing game. The original fictional city of the first
version disappeared when the real cities came in; the developer wants it back.

## Solution

Each real city becomes a **district**: five **plots** on real streets, from an outskirts panel estate to the heart of
the old town. Each plot has a fixed building type, a target height and a quality requirement, and each one inward is
harder. Plots open one after another. A finished tower stays on its plot for good, so the city visibly grows. Towers
bring residents; a **view** bonus rewards towers that see a landmark and a **skyline** bonus rewards clearly different
heights side by side. When the district reaches its residents goal, the next city opens; the district can still be
finished later. A **district map** shows the plots, their requirements, the bonuses and progress. The original
procedural city returns as **Newtown**, a seventh city outside the world map, open from the start, with Endless and
its own district.

## User Stories

1. As a player, I want each city to show a district map with five plots on real streets, so that I see where I will build.
2. As a player, I want the first plot on the outskirts and the last next to the main landmark, so that I travel into the city as I progress.
3. As a player, I want each plot to show its building type, target floors and the quality it needs before I start, so that I know what to expect.
4. As a player, I want plots to open one after another, so that the district has a clear path.
5. As a player, I want outskirts plots to be easier (fewer floors, only the target needed), so that a city starts gently.
6. As a player, I want plots near the centre to need fewer lost lives and then more perfect landings, so that the heart of the city is a real challenge.
7. As a player, I want the building type to get harder towards the centre (plain panel outside, hotel further in, the city's signature type at the heart), so that each plot plays differently.
8. As a player, I want wind and weather to grow through the district, so that the last plots feel dramatic.
9. As a player, I want each plot to have its own scene from the real streets around it (panel estates outside, baroque in the centre), so that the city changes as I move inward.
10. As a player, I want a finished tower to stay on its plot, so that my city grows.
11. As a player, I want finished towers visible from the other plots and on the district map, so that I see my skyline.
12. As a player, I want residents from each tower added to the district, so that I have one goal to aim for.
13. As a player, I want a "view" bonus for a tower whose upper floors see a landmark, shown before I build, so that placement feels meaningful.
14. As a player, I want a "skyline" bonus when neighbouring towers differ clearly in height, shown on the map, so that variety pays.
15. As a player, I want the next city to open when the district reaches its residents goal, so that good play opens it early.
16. As a player, I want to come back and finish or improve a district later, so that nothing is lost.
17. As a player, I want each plot to keep its best 1-3 stars and best height, and the district to show the sum, so that I can replay for more.
18. As a player, I want a plot I failed to stay open to retry, so that I am never stuck.
19. As a player, I want the district to come alive as it fills (more people, shops, traffic), so that progress is visible in the streets.
20. As a player, I want Endless on any opened city as before, so that I can play without goals.
21. As a player, I want Newtown, the original fictional city, open from the start with Endless and its own district, so that the classic city stays playable.
22. As a player, I want the world map to show Newtown apart from the six real cities, so that it is clear it is fictional.
23. As a returning player, I want my S0001 progress (Kosice stars) carried into the first plot of Kosice, so that an update does not cost me progress.
24. As the developer, I want plots, requirements, bonuses and the residents goal as level data, so that I can tune cities without code.
25. As the developer, I want each plot's OpenStreetMap extract generated by the same script with fixed parameters, so that scenes are reproducible.
26. As the developer, I want district rules in the deterministic core with tests, so that unlocking and bonuses are reliable.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Data:** a city definition gains `district: { goal, plots: [...] }`; a plot has `id, name, site {lat, lon},
  buildingType, target, require: { maxLivesLost?, minPerfectShare? }, windScale, weather, landmarksInView`.
  Kosice: five plots from an outskirts panel estate (e.g. Terasa or KVP) inward, the last at today's site next to the
  cathedral; targets about 12, 15, 18, 22, 26. Newtown uses the procedural city with five plots on its own grid.
- **Core:** a pure district module computes: which plots are open, whether a plot result counts (target plus its
  requirement), plot stars, residents per tower with the view and skyline bonuses, district sum of stars and residents,
  and whether the goal opens the next city. A run gets the plot's definition (type, target, wind, weather) as today's
  level. Deterministic; tested.
- **Progress store:** per plot best stars, best height, residents, counted flag; per city opened flag. New versioned
  key; Kosice S0001 progress maps onto its last (cathedral) plot or the first plot - decide in the ticket, keep it simple.
- **Scenes:** the OSM script takes a site and radius per plot and writes one compact JSON per plot (about 150-200 m
  radius); the near ring builds around the plot being played; the middle and far rings stay per city. Finished towers
  of the same city are placed at their real plot positions when they fall inside the loaded rings, and drawn as simple
  instanced towers on the far ring otherwise.
- **District map screen:** a stylised top-down map of the city with the five plots, a path from outskirts to centre,
  each plot's card (type, target, requirement, bonuses, stars), the residents bar towards the goal, Play on the open
  plot. Fits the UI system of S0001 ticket 18 and the world map of ticket 20.
- **Newtown:** the procedural city of `js/world.js` (`nearProc`) becomes a city entry with id `newtown`, flagged
  fictional, open from the start; its plots sit on its own streets.
- **Endless** keeps one tower on the city's first or central plot, unchanged rules.
- **Originality:** no grid, no neighbour-type rules; plots are real places; bonuses are view and skyline only.

## Testing Decisions

- Core (Vitest): plot opening order; a result counts only with target and requirement; residents with view and skyline
  bonuses; district goal opens the next city; replays keep the best; progress migration from S0001 keys; determinism.
- Browser checks: load each Kosice plot scene, screenshots at start and floor 8, perf at 2560x1440 DPR 2 on High,
  flicker, no console errors; district map screenshots on desktop and phone width.
- Prior art: `tests/core/*.test.js`, `tests/browser/check.mjs`.

## Out of Scope

- The other five real cities' districts (they follow in S0001 tickets 21-30, now with five plots each).
- Neighbour or adjacency rules of any kind.
- Multiplayer or sharing cities.

## Further Notes

- Depends on S0001 tickets 18 (UI system) and 20 (world map) for the screens' look; the core and data work can start now.
- Size budget: about 2-3 MB of OSM data per city on top of the S0001 budget.
