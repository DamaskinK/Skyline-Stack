# Music and sound effects: candidate list

Research for ticket S0001 15. Nothing was downloaded; every row below was read on the asset page itself (licence field
checked there, not in a search listing). Researched 2026-11-22.

## How to read this

- **Licence** is the value on the asset page. `CC0` needs no credit. `CC-BY 4.0` needs the credit line given in the row notes.
- **Length** is the value on the page where one is stated. Where the page gives none, the length is marked `~` and is
  estimated from the file size (wav at 44.1 kHz stereo 16 bit, mp3 at 128 kbps, ogg at about 160 kbps). Check it when you
  listen. Estimates can be off by a third.
- OpenGameArt (OGA) blocks plain scripted fetches, so its pages were read in a normal browser pane. Freesound shows the
  licence on the sound page, but a free account is needed to download.
- Sources that did not work out: **FreePD is permanently closed** (site notice, 2008-2025), so no FreePD tracks are
  listed. Free Music Archive was not mined: most of its tracks are CC-BY-NC or CC-BY-ND, which are not allowed here.
- Search is limited to what indexes well. There are surely more tracks; these are the best verified fits.
